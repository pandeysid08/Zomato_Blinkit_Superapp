## Not public (modeled in this repo)
Zomato–Blinkit customer overlap, cross-category basket attach rate, A/B test outcomes, Kano survey responses and feature effort estimates. `analysis/` marks every such input as an assumption. Verify the metrics against S5 before quoting them.
