Zomato × Blinkit: building India's lifestyle superapp

Product analytics case study · Siddharth Pandey, IIT Bhubaneswar

Can one integrated experience raise AOV, retention and adoption across Eternal's food delivery and quick commerce businesses? This study frames the hypotheses, prioritizes features and plans a staged rollout.

Rollout
